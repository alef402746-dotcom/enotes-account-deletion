02

KNOW WHAT HAPPENS

Your data, explained.

Removed from the active database

  • Your account, profile, school details and avatar
  • Synced bookmarks, progress, recall cards and annotations
  • Your follows, votes, online presence and study-room membership
  • Messages sent by you and direct messages addressed to you
  • Your community text, images and author identity
  • Account-linked AI usage counters

What may remain

  • Deleted placeholders, thread timestamps and classmates’ replies
  • Other students’ messages and shared study circles
  • Copies, quotations or exports held by other people
  • Backups and security logs until their applicable retention periods expire
  • External providers’ records under their own retention policies

Exact backup and log retention periods are still being verified. We do not promise immediate erasure of every historical copy. Deleting eNotes does not delete your Google account.
